# Planet

The game takes place on a shattered planet known as **Aetheris**.

Thousands of years ago, Aetheris was one united world ruled by an advanced civilization capable of controlling unimaginable energy.

Everything changed after an event known as **The Collapse**.

The planet fractured into massive floating landmasses called **Fragments**.

Each Fragment developed differently over centuries.

Some became lush forests.

Some froze forever.

Some burned under endless volcanoes.

Others became ruled by mysterious Ancient Machines.

---

# The Seven Fragments

## 1. Greenheart
Dense forests, rivers, abandoned villages.

Difficulty: Easy

Purpose:
The player's starting region.

---

## 2. Frostveil

Frozen mountains and ancient laboratories.

Difficulty:
Easy-Medium

Secrets:
Old research facilities hidden beneath glaciers.

---

## 3. Ember Wastes

A desert filled with ruins and giant sandstorms.

Difficulty:
Medium

---

## 4. Iron Dominion

Factories controlled entirely by Ancient Machines.

Difficulty:
Medium-Hard

---

## 5. Storm Peaks

Floating islands surrounded by constant lightning.

Difficulty:
Hard

Traversal:
Heavy parkour.

---

## 6. Abyss Frontier

Dark corrupted lands where reality begins breaking apart.

Difficulty:
Very Hard

---

## 7. Core Fragment

The center of the world.

Nobody has entered for centuries.

This is where The Collapse began.

Final Area.
