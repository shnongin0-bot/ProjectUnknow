# Enemy System

## Vision

Enemies should make every exploration dangerous, memorable, and rewarding.

Every region contains unique creatures with different behaviors and combat styles.

---

## Humans

- Raiders
- Mercenaries
- Bandits
- Hunters

---

## Creatures

- Wolves
- Giant Spiders
- Mutated Bears
- Cave Crawlers
- Stone Golems

---

## Ancient Machines

- Scout Drone
- Guardian Walker
- Flying Sentinel
- Heavy Destroyer

## Bosses

Each region has one major boss.

Examples:

- Titan Guardian
- Sand Colossus
- Frozen Leviathan
- Ancient King
- Sky Serpent

- Easy
- Normal
- Elite

# Enemy AI

Enemies can:

- Patrol
- Search
- Hear Sounds
- Investigate
- Take Cover
- Call Reinforcements
- Flee when weak

Enemies drop:

- Resources

- Armor
- Crafting Parts
- Ancient Cores
- Rare Artifacts

# World Events

Random events may occur:

- Bandit Ambush
- Machine Invasion
- Monster Migration
- Boss Appearance
