# Project Unknown

# Character Design Document

---

# Main Protagonist

## Code Name

Unknown

The player's real identity is hidden at the beginning of the game.

They awaken beneath the ruins of an ancient facility with no memories.

Only one artifact remains:

The Core Compass.

---

# Personality

- Calm
- Curious
- Brave
- Determined
- Quiet

The player's personality is shaped by the choices they make.

---

# Companion AI

## Name

ECHO

An ancient AI hidden inside the Core Compass.

Originally created to guide explorers before The Collapse.

ECHO provides:

- Navigation
- Puzzle hints
- Historical records
- Upgrade analysis

Over time, ECHO begins recovering its own forgotten memories.

---

# Main Villain

## Name

The Architect

The final surviving creator of the Aether Core Network.

Nobody knows whether The Architect is human, machine, or something else.

The Architect believes humanity failed and wants to rebuild the world through machines.

---

# Friendly Factions

## The Wanderers

Travelers exploring the Fragments.

Help the player with quests and supplies.

---

## The Keepers

Scholars preserving knowledge from the old civilization.

They know more about The Collapse than anyone.

---

# Enemy Factions

## Machine Legion

Ancient robots corrupted after The Collapse.

Guard old cities and attack intruders.

---

## The Hollow

Humans transformed by unstable Aether energy.

Aggressive and unpredictable.
