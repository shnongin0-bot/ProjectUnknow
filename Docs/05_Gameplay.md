# Project Unknown

# Gameplay Design Document

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

1. Explore the world
2. Discover ruins and secrets
3. Fight enemies
4. Collect resources
5. Upgrade equipment
6. Unlock new abilities
7. Complete missions
8. Defeat bosses
9. Reach new Fragments

---

# Gameplay Pillars

## Exploration

- Open World
- Hidden caves
- Ancient ruins
- Secret puzzles
- Treasure hunting

---

## Combat

Fast-paced third-person combat featuring:

- Melee weapons
- Ranged weapons
- Dodging
- Parrying
- Special abilities

---

## Parkour

Movement is a major feature.

Players can:

- Sprint
- Slide
- Wall Run
- Wall Jump
- Climb
- Vault
- Grapple
- Zipline

---

## Building

Players can build:

- Small shelters
- Storage
- Crafting stations
- Defensive walls
- Watch towers

---

## Vehicles

Travel using:

- Motorbike
- Buggy
- Hover Bike
- Ancient Airship

---

## Progression

Players improve by:

- Leveling up
- Unlocking skills
- Crafting better gear
- Discovering ancient technology

---

## Inventory

Collect:

- Resources
- Weapons
- Armor
- Artifacts
- Blueprints
- Healing items

---

## Boss Battles

Each Fragment contains one major boss.

Bosses unlock:

- Core Keys
- New abilities
- Story progression

---

## Multiplayer (Future)

Possible features:

- Co-op exploration
- Shared base building
- World events
- Trading

---
