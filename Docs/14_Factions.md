# Factions

## Vision

The world of Aetheris is shaped by groups with different beliefs about the future.

Every faction has its own goals, allies, enemies, and rewards.

---

# The Keepers

Role:
Protect ancient knowledge.

Belief:
Knowledge should never be lost again.

Base:
The Grand Archive

Leader:
High Archivist Elyra

---

# The Wanderers

Role:
Explorers and traders.

Belief:
Every Fragment hides new opportunities.

Base:
Moving Caravan Cities

Leader:
Captain Rowan

---

# Iron Legion

Role:
Militant survivors.

Belief:
Strength ensures survival.

Base:
Iron Bastion

Leader:
Commander Kael

---

# The Hollow

Role:
Humans corrupted by unstable Aether energy.

Belief:
None remain. They act on instinct.

Danger Level:
Extreme

---

# Ancient Machine Network

Role:
Autonomous machine civilization.

Directive:
Protect the Core.

Some machines are hostile.
Some are neutral.
Some may become allies.

---
