# Ancient Machines

## Vision

Ancient Machines are the greatest mystery in Project Unknown.

Nobody knows who created them or why they still operate after centuries.

Some protect forgotten cities.
Some hunt anything that moves.
Others simply watch...

---

# Machine Classes

## Scout

Small flying machine.

Purpose:
- Detect intruders
- Scan ruins
- Alert nearby machines

---

## Guardian

Medium combat machine.

Weapons:
- Energy Blade
- Plasma Cannon
- Shield Generator

---

## Walker

Large four-legged machine.

Purpose:
- Heavy defense
- Long-range attacks
- Area control

---

## Titan

Massive boss machine.

Abilities:

- Earthquake Slam
- Missile Barrage
- Energy Beam
- Shield Phase

---

# Machine Types

- Ground Units
- Flying Units
- Naval Units
- Underground Units

---

# Weakness System

Machines have weak points:

- Power Core
- Cooling System
- Optical Sensor
- Energy Battery

Destroying these weak points changes their behavior.

---

# Machine AI

Machines can:

- Patrol ruins
- Guard ancient technology
- Hunt players
- Fight other creatures
- Defend ancient cities

---

# Ancient Technology

Machines use:
