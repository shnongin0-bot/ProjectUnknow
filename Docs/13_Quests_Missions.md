# Quests & Missions

## Vision

Every quest should reveal something meaningful about the world, its people, or the player's journey.

Players should rarely feel like they are completing repetitive tasks.

---

# Quest Types

## Main Story

Advances the main narrative.

Rewards:

- Story Progression
- Core Keys
- New Regions
- Major Upgrades

---

## Side Quests

Focused on NPCs, settlements, and world-building.

Rewards:

- XP
- Equipment
- Resources
- Reputation

---

## Exploration Quests

Encourage discovery.

Objectives:

- Discover ruins
- Climb towers
- Solve puzzles
- Find hidden locations

---

## Machine Hunts

Hunt dangerous Ancient Machines.

Rewards:

- Machine Parts
- Blueprints
- Legendary Gear

---

## World Events

Dynamic events appear randomly.

Examples:

- Settlement Under Attack
- Machine Invasion
- Sandstorm Rescue
- Lost Caravan
- Ancient Signal

---

# Reputation

Factions remember your actions.

Helping them unlocks:

- Discounts
- Equipment
- New quests
- Special companions

---

# Quest Rewards

Players receive:

- XP
- Currency
- Rare Materials
- Weapons
- Armor
- Vehicles
- Cosmetics

---

# Design Philosophy

- Every quest should tell a story.
- Avoid repetitive "collect 10 items" missions.
- Encourage exploration over grinding.
