# Skills & Progression

## Vision

Progression should reward exploration, mastery, and player choice.

There are no fixed classes.

Players build their own playstyle.

---

# Experience

Players earn XP by:

- Exploring
- Completing quests
- Defeating enemies
- Discovering ruins
- Solving puzzles

---

# Skill Trees

## Combat

Focuses on:

- Damage
- Critical Hits
- Perfect Parry
- Weapon Mastery
- Ultimate Skills

---

## Movement

Unlocks:

- Double Jump
- Longer Wall Run
- Faster Climbing
- Air Dash
- Improved Grapple

---

## Survival

Improves:

- Health
- Stamina
- Resource Gathering
- Crafting Speed
- Carry Capacity

---

## Technology

Allows players to:

- Hack Machines
- Upgrade Ancient Gear
- Build Advanced Structures
- Unlock Lost Technology

---

# Level Cap

Maximum Level: 100

---

# Rewards

Every level grants:

- Skill Points
- Attribute Points
- Cosmetic Unlocks

---

# Attributes

- Health
- Stamina
- Strength
- Agility
- Intelligence
- Engineering

---

# End Game

Players can eventually unlock hybrid builds by combining different skill trees.
