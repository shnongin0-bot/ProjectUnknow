# Project Unknown

# Story Document

---

# Overview

Project Unknown tells the story of a forgotten world destroyed by an event known only as **The Collapse**.

The player awakens with no memory inside a mysterious underground chamber.

The world above has become broken into floating Fragments.

Ancient Machines roam the ruins.

Nobody remembers what truly happened.

Only one question remains:

**What caused The Collapse?**

---

# Before The Collapse

Thousands of years ago...

The planet Aetheris was united under one civilization.

Humanity had discovered a limitless energy source called **Aether Core Energy**.

This energy solved every problem.

No wars.

No disease.

Unlimited technology.

Massive floating cities.

Artificial intelligence.

Machine armies.

Humanity believed they had reached perfection.

But they were wrong.

---

# The Collapse

Scientists attempted one final experiment.

They tried connecting every Aether Core into one giant network.

For a brief moment...

Everything worked.

Then something answered.

The entire planet was engulfed in white light.

Mountains split apart.

Cities floated into the sky.

Reality itself fractured.

Millions disappeared instantly.

The surviving machines became corrupted.

Nobody knows what was awakened.

---

# Ancient Machines

The Machines were originally built to protect humanity.

After The Collapse...

Their programming became corrupted.

Some still defend ancient cities.

Others hunt every living creature.

A few appear to remember the old world.

---

# The Player

The protagonist wakes alone.

No memories.

No identity.

Only one strange object remains.

An ancient device known as **The Core Compass**.

The Compass reacts to hidden technology scattered across the world.

As the player explores...

Fragments of forgotten memories slowly return.

---

# Main Goal

Travel across all Seven Fragments.

Recover the lost Core Keys.

Reach the Core Fragment.

Discover the truth behind The Collapse.

Choose the future of humanity.
