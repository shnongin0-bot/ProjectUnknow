# Lore Bible

## Purpose

This document is the single source of truth for the history, mythology, and rules of the world.

Everything in Project Unknown must follow this document.

---

# Timeline

## Age of Discovery

Humanity discovers Aether Core Energy.

Technology rapidly advances.

---

## Age of Unity

The world becomes one civilization.

Disease and famine disappear.

Ancient Machines are created.

---

## The Great Experiment

Scientists connect every Aether Core.

The experiment succeeds...

for a few seconds.

---

## The Collapse

Reality fractures.

The planet breaks into floating Fragments.

Millions disappear.

Ancient Machines activate emergency protocols.

---

## Age of Silence

Centuries pass.

Civilizations collapse.

Knowledge is lost.

---

## Present Day

The player awakens.

The truth has been forgotten.

Only fragments remain.

---

# Universal Rules

- Ancient technology cannot be easily reproduced.
- Every Fragment has its own ecosystem.
- Aether Energy powers advanced technology.
- Ancient Machines follow their original directives unless corrupted.
- The Heart Core is the center of the world's mystery.
