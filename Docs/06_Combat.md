# Combat System

## Vision

Combat in Project Unknown should feel cinematic, strategic, and rewarding.

Players should rely on movement, timing, and skill instead of button mashing.

---

# Combat Pillars

- Fast
- Responsive
- Tactical
- Challenging
- Rewarding

---

# Melee Combat

Weapons include:

- Sword
- Spear
- Axe
- Hammer
- Dual Blades

Each weapon has:

- Light Attack
- Heavy Attack
- Charged Attack
- Running Attack
- Air Attack

---

# Ranged Combat

- Bow
- Crossbow
- Energy Rifle
- Ancient Launcher

---

# Defensive Mechanics

Players can:

- Dodge
- Roll
- Sprint
- Block
- Perfect Parry

Perfect timing opens enemies for counter attacks.

---

# Combo System

Example:

Light → Light → Heavy

Heavy → Dodge → Heavy

Jump → Air Slash → Ground Slam

---

# Special Skills

Every weapon unlocks unique skills.

Examples:

- Shockwave Slash
- Earth Splitter
- Phantom Dash
- Gravity Pull
- Flame Strike

---

# Enemy Types

- Scout
- Soldier
- Hunter
- Heavy Unit
- Ancient Guardian

Each enemy has different attack patterns.

---

# Boss Battles

Bosses have:

- Multiple phases
- Weak points
- Special attacks
- Cinematic finishers

---

# Combat Progression

Players unlock:

- New skills
- Weapon upgrades
- Passive abilities
- Ultimate attacks

---

# Difficulty

Combat should reward:

- Timing
- Positioning
- Observation
- Strategy

instead of high damage alone.

---

# Combat Themes

- Skill
- Precision
- Speed
- Risk vs Reward
- Mastery
